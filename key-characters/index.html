KEY CHARACTERS

Intro Key Characters World Building

Rudo Surebrec

A fierce and stubborn "cleaner" who was falsely accused of murder and cast down into the Abyss. Driven by a burning desire for vengeance against the Sky People, he possesses the rare ability to draw out the latent powers of everyday objects. Armed with his treasured gloves and a relentless spirit, he fights to survive the harsh wasteland and reclaim his life.

Enjin Pharjin

The charismatic and seasoned Giver who acts as Rudo’s guide and mentor in the Abyss. He wields a massive umbrella Jinki called "Umbrellaman" with devastating skill and tactical precision. Beneath his laid-back demeanor and eccentric fashion lies a deeply protective leader who holds a strong moral compass. His name is also obnoxiously long, so I shortened it to the first and last name.

Zodyl Typhon

The enigmatic and ruthless leader of the Vandals, dedicated to upending the current world order. He harbors a deep-seated hatred for the status quo and commands his faction with cold, calculating authority. Driven by a mysterious philosophy, he stands as a major threat to both the cleaners and the delicate balance of the Abyss.

Tamsy Caines

A highly competent and observant member of the cleaners who brings technical expertise to the frontline. He possesses a sharp analytical mind and an intricate understanding of the mechanics behind Jinki weapons. While he often acts as a voice of reason, his calculating nature keeps everyone guessing about his deepest motivations.

Zanka Nijiku

Zanka Nijiku is a fierce, hard-working fighter who masks a deeply reliable nature beneath a blunt, abrasive exterior and a notorious "bad attitude." Lacking natural genius but possessing immense tactical brilliance, he relies on relentless training and absolute discipline to match the most gifted warriors. Armed with a spike-and-vibration-powered polearm, he serves as a strict, fiercely protective mentor and an invaluable ally who will always have your back in a fight.

Cthoni Andor

A physically imposing and fiercely loyal warrior who uses overwhelming strength to crush his enemies. He acts as a heavy hitter in combat, diving headfirst into danger to shield his comrades from harm. While he can appear intimidating, his actions are driven by a simple, straightforward desire to protect his chosen family.
