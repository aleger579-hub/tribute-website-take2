WORLD BUILDING

Intro Key Characters World Building

GUIDE

There are many different areas in the world of gachiakuta, which is divided into two distinct regions: the Sphere, a clean, floating city in the sky where the wealthy elite live, and the Ground below, a massive wasteland where all of the Sphere's trash and criminals are dropped. The two worlds are separated by a thick boundary layer of clouds and falling waste, creating a harsh environment for the people living on the surface. From the high-tech urban landscape above to the various Wards and junk piles below, each area is defined by how it handles resources and refuse.

LOCATIONS

The Sphere

The Sphere is a pristine, high-tech city that floats high in the sky, serving as an isolated paradise for the wealthy elite. The inhabitants enjoy a clean, orderly lifestyle entirely detached from the world below, completely hidden away behind strict social stratification. However, this perfection comes at a cost, as the city maintains its purity by dumping all of its massive quantities of garbage and criminals over the edge.

The Ground

The Gorund is the brutal, toxic wasteland on the surface world where all of the Sphere's discarded trash and outcasts accumulate. Life here is a constant struggle for survival against extreme pollution, harsh weather, and the dangerous, mutated monsters known as Trash Beasts. The inhabitants, known as Groundlings, are forced to scavenge through endless heaps of refuse just to find resources and build makeshift settlements.

The Border

The Border is the treacherous, cloud-filled drop zone that separates the sky-bound Sphere from the surface of the Pit. It serves as an atmospheric barrier filled with heavy smog, falling debris, and toxic gasses that make crossing it nearly impossible. It is also the hunting ground for a colossal, terrifying entity known as the Watchman, ensuring that anything cast down remains exiled forever.

The Cleaner's Agency

The Cleaners Headquarters is the fortified base of operations located within the Pit's East Ward that Rudo visits early in his journey. This facility serves as the central hub for the Cleaners, a specialized organization dedicated to hunting Trash Beasts and reclaiming useful materials from the waste. Inside, members study the mysterious power of Jinki, store specialized gear, and coordinate dangerous missions to keep the local human settlements safe.

KEY GROUND WARDS & ZONES

  • The Safe Zones: Walled towns with clean, filtered air and basic living infrastructure.
  • The Polluted Zones: Areas heavily contaminated by toxic trash where people must wear gas masks to survive.
  • The Wards: Four distinct geographical regions of the Ground, including the East Ward where Canvas Town and the Cleaners are located.
  • No Man's Lands: Highly dangerous, unlivable areas overrun by toxic waste and deadly environmental hazards.
